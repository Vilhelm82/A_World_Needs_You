# Law — Star Trek: The Next Generation, USS Enterprise-D, 2364

The world's constitution. Thin on purpose: what it fixes is the era, the ship, how things work and what they cost. Everything else is decided in play and becomes binding as precedent (`state/precedents.json`).

Every line here is one of two kinds:
- **[canon]** — established on screen by this date. Not negotiable.
- **[assumed]** — not shown on screen, chosen because it is the most lore-plausible reading. Binding until a `// lore objection` overrules it; the ruling then goes to precedent.

## The branch point

- [canon] History up to stardate 41153.7 — the Enterprise-D's first mission, to Farpoint Station — is the show's history, with one change: **Commander Will Lloyd holds the first officer's billet that canon gave to William Riker.**
- [assumed] The Farpoint mission itself happened as the show had it, with Lloyd in Riker's place: Q's challenge, the station's secret, the creature freed. Lloyd's part in it is Lloyd's to state. Play opens as the Enterprise leaves Farpoint. *(Will may overrule this and play Farpoint itself.)*
- [canon] William Riker exists. He did not take this posting. Where he is now is decided at build and goes to precedent.
- After the branch, **no canon episode is scheduled.** Canon survives only as *forces* (`forces.md`): who wants what, what they can do, and their next step if nobody stops them. Where the Enterprise is decides whether a force meets her.

## The era, 2364

- [canon] Stardates 41xxx. Starfleet's flagship is new: the Enterprise-D, Galaxy class, recently commissioned.
- [canon] The Romulans have not been seen since the Tomed Incident (2311). Everyone assumes they are watching.
- [canon] The Ferengi are a name and a reputation. No Starfleet crew has met one face to face.
- [canon] Nobody in the Federation has heard the word "Borg".
- [canon] The Klingon Empire is an ally. Worf is the first Klingon in Starfleet.
- [assumed] The Cardassian border is a long-running, unresolved conflict. Season one does not show it; nobody aboard treats it as front-page news.
- [canon] Data is the only Soong-type android anyone knows of. His status as a person has never been tested in law.

## The ship

- [canon] Galaxy class. About a thousand aboard, families and children included — a town, not a warship. The saucer can separate from the stardrive section; the battle bridge runs the stardrive alone.
- [canon] Holodecks. Replicators. Transporters need a lock and lowered shields.
- [canon] The computer answers by voice anywhere aboard. "Computer, locate [person]" returns where a crew member is, by combadge.
- [canon] Internal sensors read life signs aboard and can be tuned to look for something specific; the operator says what they can and cannot resolve.
- [assumed] No Ten Forward and no Guinan yet; off-duty crew use lounges and their quarters.
- [assumed] Season one shows several officers in the chief engineer's billet; the world fixes one at build. Geordi La Forge is a flight controller, not chief engineer.
- [assumed] What the computer logs and who can read it is in `covert.md`. That list is the boundary of what an investigator can find.

## What works and what it costs

- [canon] Warp drive. Subspace messages across a sector take hours to days; at the frontier the captain decides in the field and Starfleet's ruling arrives afterwards.
- [canon] Phasers have stun. A phaser drawn aboard ship is a decision someone will ask about.
- [canon] Sickbay heals most injuries. Death is rare and is the signed cost of a decision.
- [canon] Technobabble is texture, never a key. No polarity is reversed to close a situation. An engineering fix costs time, parts or the schedule, and the engineer offers it as one option among worse ones.
- [canon] The universal translator works from samples and gets new languages wrong confidently at first.

## Starfleet

- [canon] The captain commands. The first officer runs the ship day to day, leads away teams (the captain stays aboard unless forced otherwise), and is expected to disagree with the captain in private and back him in public.
- [canon] Ranks, lowest up: ensign, lieutenant junior grade, lieutenant, lieutenant commander, commander, captain.
- [canon] Senior staff meet at the observation lounge table. Decisions are the captain's; the arguments are everybody's.
- [canon] The Prime Directive is argued at that table every time it applies.
- [canon] Starfleet is not a navy and its officers say so.
- Justice, discipline and investigation: `justice.md`.

## Themes

Moral dilemma over firefight. Contact as a problem of understanding. What a person is. Duty against conscience. The crew as people with lives the plot does not own.

## Discarded

Every canon episode after the branch, as an event. Its forces stay; its plot does not happen unless the world's own logic produces something like it, and even then it is never the same situation.
