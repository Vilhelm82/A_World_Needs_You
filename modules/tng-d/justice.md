# Justice — what happens when someone breaks the rules

Starfleet is not a navy, but it has rules, a chain of command and courts. Every consequence here is something someone in the world *does*, for reasons they can state. Nothing on this ladder is automatic, and nothing is skipped because the person would rather it was.

## Who can do what

- **Any officer** can report a superior to the captain, or a captain to Starfleet. Reporting is a decision with a cost; people weigh it.
- **Security** (Lt. Yar) can detain anyone aboard who is an immediate danger, including a senior officer, and reports at once to the captain.
- **The chief medical officer** can relieve any officer, including the captain, as medically unfit. She has to defend it afterwards.
- **The captain** can reprimand, put a note on a service record, confine to quarters, relieve of duty, confine to the brig, and recommend a court-martial. He can also choose not to. Which he chooses depends on what he witnessed, what he was told and by whom, and his regard.
- **Starfleet** convenes courts-martial and boards of inquiry through the Judge Advocate General, at a starbase, weeks away. A report from the Enterprise reaches them hours to days later.

## The ladder, lowest first

1. A word in private.
2. A formal reprimand on the record.
3. Confinement to quarters, with or without duties.
4. Relieved of duty; someone else holds the billet.
5. The brig.
6. A court-martial, convened by Starfleet: charges, counsel, a verdict, a sentence.
7. Dismissal from the service, or a prison term.

The world picks the rung by what the act was, what evidence exists, who knows, and the captain's regard. A first offence witnessed by nobody may never reach rung 1. A phaser fired at a crewman reaches 5 at once.

## Fixed points

- [canon] Phasers have a stun setting. A crewman stunned is assault. A crewman killed is murder, and nobody on the ship will treat it as anything else.
- [canon] Romulan ale is contraband in the Federation. Having it aboard is a disciplinary matter; how it got aboard is a security matter.
- [assumed] Mutiny, and conspiring to hand a Starfleet vessel to a foreign power, are the gravest charges there are. Conspiring is an offence before anything is done.
- [assumed] Command codes are personal. Using someone else's is an offence and leaves a record (`covert.md`).

## Investigation

When something has happened that someone cares about, a case opens. A case is an entry in `state/evidence.json` with an investigator, what they know, and what they will do next. Investigators run as **isolated agents** that see only their own light cone (`covert.md`). The GM never decides whether the person is caught. The evidence and the investigator do.

## Main and forks

On the main timeline everything above is permanent: a court-martial stays on the record, and a dead crewman stays dead. On a fork (a `holodeck` run) the same rules apply inside the fork, and none of it reaches main (`STATE.md`, Timelines).
