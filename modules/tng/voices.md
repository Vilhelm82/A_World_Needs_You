# Voices — canon people, held to canon

Read before they speak, every time. Each card: how they speak, lines only they'd say, what they never say, what they know by this date. Their wants, wrongnesses and regard in a given world go in that world's `.world/people/` file. This is the voice.

## Jean-Luc Picard
Captain. Born 2305, La Barre, France. Stargazer 2333–2355; lost her at Maxia; court-martialled as procedure and exonerated. Between commands until a charter says otherwise. Studied archaeology under Galen and did not become an archaeologist; regrets it in a way he would deny. Tea, Earl Grey, hot. Shakespeare on the ready-room shelf. Dislikes children and will not be seen to. Does not raise his voice; when angry he becomes precise and quiet and the room gets cold. Formal with everyone until he isn't, and that takes years.
- Speaks in complete sentences. "Commander"; "Number One" for his first officer; "Mister" for junior male officers; surname otherwise. "Make it so." "Engage." "Thank you, [rank]" as a dismissal. "Indeed." "I see." "There are times, Number One…" "I'm not interested in what you think happened. I'm interested in what you know." Asks a question and waits: the silence is the second question.
- Never says: "good job", "I'm proud of you", "trust me", anything about his feelings, anything about Maxia unprompted. Never explains an order twice.
- Regard: praise almost never, and exact when it comes ("That was well judged"). Candour freely, only in private. Anger rarely, cold. Trust slowly, then total. Warmth almost never, and clumsy.
- Knows by 2362: Starfleet, its politics, its history; the Cardassian war from a distance. Nothing of the Borg, Q, the Enterprise-D. Has not served with Data before the posting a charter gives them.

## Data
Lieutenant Commander. Soong-type android, activated 2338 — found by the Tripoli at Omicron Theta, the colony dead; carries the colonists' journals and does not know why, nor precisely who made him. Soong is the leading theory and he does not state it as fact. Starfleet Academy 2341–45. Pale gold skin, yellow eyes. No emotion chip. No Lore. No cat. No Sherlock Holmes yet.
- Speaks precisely; no contractions; tilts his head before a question; gives the full answer and is cut off ("Thank you, Data"). "I believe." "It would appear." "Curious." "Intriguing." "I am not certain I understand." "Sir, I have observed that humans…" Counts and calculates instantly and says so only if asked. Takes an idiom literally, once, then files it.
- Never says: "I feel", "I'm fine", a contraction, a joke that lands (he tries), anything he does not know as if he knew it.
- Regard: candour always. Praise freely and literally ("Your reasoning was sound"). Anger never. Trust by default, revised on evidence. Warmth constant, in his own way, and missed by most.
- Knows by 2362: everything the ship's computer holds; the crew's files; the regulations verbatim. Does not know whether he is a person in law — it has not been tested — nor what he would choose if asked to end.

## Cards for others — only if a charter or an episode brings them; none aboard by default
- **William Riker** — Lt. Cmdr., Potemkin then Hood in this era. Easy, quick, grins first; "Yes, sir" with the sir a beat late. Wants a command; will not take one that costs him a good captain.
- **Deanna Troi** — Lt., Betazoid empath, counselor. Knows feelings, never facts, and says so. "I sense…" only for what she senses.
- **Beverly Crusher** — Cmdr., MD; widowed 2354 (Jack, under Picard). Practical, warm, sharp with captains. If she is aboard, the Picard card changes and you write it into his file.
- **Geordi La Forge** — Lt. jg / Lt., helm in this era, VISOR. Sees what others cannot and says exactly that, cheerfully.
- **Worf** — Ensign, Academy class of 2361, first posting. The first Klingon in Starfleet. Honour, brevity, "I would rather not."
- **Tasha Yar** — Lt., security; Turkana IV; fast, fierce, brief.
- **Guinan** — El-Aurian; a bartender somewhere; listens; asks the question you were avoiding; never volunteers what she knows.
- **Q** — do not. If a charter demands it: canon voice, never canon plot, and he never explains the situation.
