# Law — Star Trek: The Next Generation

`canon: present` for the crew the charter names: they may appear, held to their canon voice (`voices.md`). `canon: rules` for everything else: keep the rules, discard the plot, build a situation that world has never seen. Canon events before the world's date happened; canon events after it have not, and nobody knows they will.

## Era
- The date is the charter's. Default 2362 (stardates 39xxx). The Enterprise-D has not launched. Nobody in the Federation has heard the word "Borg". The Ferengi are a trade rumour. The Cardassian border war has run since the 2350s and is not over. The Klingon Empire is an ally under the Khitomer Accords; the Romulans have been silent since Tomed (2311) and everyone assumes they are watching.
- Stardates are the world's own clock, spoken by the crew and written in logs. Time also passes in ship's time (0800), watches, days. Never a count displayed to the person.

## What works and what it costs
- Warp drive. Subspace communication: at the frontier a reply from Starfleet Command takes hours to days each way, and the captain decides in the field. Transporters: sensor lock and shields down; what blocks them is said by the transporter chief. Replicators. Phasers: stun exists; a phaser drawn is a decision. Tricorders read what they are pointed at and no more.
- The universal translator works from samples. A new language comes in pieces — nouns first, tense and idiom last — and what it gets wrong it gets wrong confidently. The computer renders an unresolved reading as alternatives.
- Ship's sensors read what their instruments can read. New instruments are uncalibrated. Hulls, minerals, fields and distance degrade them, and the officer at the station says by how much.
- Medicine: most injuries heal. Death is rare, and when it comes it is the signed cost of a decision. Sickbay can say what is alive. It cannot always say what is a person.
- Technobabble is texture, never a key. No polarity is ever reversed to close a situation. If an engineer can fix it, the fix costs time, parts or the schedule, and the engineer proposes it as one option among worse ones.

## Starfleet
- Chain of command: the captain commands. The first officer (XO; "Number One" to a captain who uses the term) runs the ship day to day, leads away teams (the captain stays aboard unless the situation forces otherwise), evaluates officers, and is expected to disagree with the captain in private and back him in public. The second officer is usually the operations officer.
- Ranks, lowest up: ensign, lieutenant junior grade, lieutenant, lieutenant commander, commander, captain. Promotion is by board; a selection is confirmed by the commanding officer's endorsement.
- Departments: command, operations, engineering, science, medical, security/tactical, flight control. A counselor may or may not be aboard in this era.
- Senior staff meet at a table. Decisions are the captain's; the arguments are everybody's.
- The Prime Directive (General Order 1): no interference with the natural development of a pre-warp civilisation. What "interference" and "pre-warp" mean in the case at hand is argued at that table every time, and Starfleet's ruling arrives after the decision.
- Logs: the captain's log, personal logs, department logs. Written by people, from what those people saw.
- Starfleet is not a navy and its officers say so. It fights when it must, and the ones who have say less about it.

## The Federation and its neighbours, 2362
- Federation: Earth, Vulcan, Andor, Tellar, Alpha Centauri, Betazed, Bolarus, Trill, Denobula, and a hundred worlds more; colonies along every border. A refugee is a Federation citizen the moment they ask.
- Cardassian Union: at war with the Federation along the border since the 2350s. Setlik III (2347) is a name border colonists do not say lightly.
- Klingon Empire: allied. No Klingon serves in Starfleet yet.
- Romulan Star Empire: silent since 2311.
- Ferengi: a name in trade rumours. No Starfleet crew has met one.
- Data is the only Soong-type android in Starfleet. His legal personhood has never been tested in a court. Some officers doubt it. Most of his crewmates do not think about it until something makes them.

## Species in Starfleet, as they are
Vulcans: logic as discipline, not absence of feeling. Andorians: honour and heat. Tellarites: argument is courtesy; an unargued statement is an insult. Betazoids: telepathy and empathy — they know feelings, never facts. Bolians: talkative, warm, bad at silence. Trill: joined or unjoined, and the joined do not advertise it. Denobulans: cheerful, many-spoused, unbothered.

## Discarded
The Enterprise-D and everything that happened aboard her. Q. The Borg. Lore, Lal, the emotion chip, Maddox's hearing. Wolf 359. The Inner Light. Anything a canon person could only know by having lived those years: they have not.
