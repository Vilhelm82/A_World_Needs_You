# Sources, scope and deliberate simplifications

Checked 21 September 2026. The first case uses fictional contract terms and
explicit simulation rules. No fictional rule below is represented as legislation.
No school competition case script or scoring rubric has been copied.

## Verified background sources, not additional governing rules

- Law Society of NSW, Mock Trial Competition:
  https://www.lawsociety.com.au/Legal-Communities/High-School-Students/Mock-Trial-Competition
  The current page describes student civil/criminal scenarios, party roles and
  volunteer judicial officers. Used as background for an advocacy simulation.
- Local Court of NSW, Civil Jurisdiction:
  https://dcj.nsw.gov.au/content/dcj/ctsd/localcourt/local-court/about-us/jurisdictions0/civil-jurisdiction.html
  Its public guide explains civil money/property disputes and the balance of
  probabilities standard. P1 adopts that basic standard, not the full jurisdiction.
- Judicial Commission of NSW, Civil Trials Bench Book landing page:
  https://www.judcom.nsw.gov.au/civil-trials-bench-book
  The current September 2026 page identifies the bench book and its limited,
  non-encyclopaedic purpose. It is a future verification resource, not a claim
  that this prototype implements its contents.

## Simulation choices

The fictional court, P1-P12, sequence of witnesses, reduced objection system,
hearsay categories, record formats, sole civil claim and remedies are simulation
simplifications. The case brief and E01 supply the relevant fictional agreement.
Existing real-world doctrines outside that packet do not enter by improvisation.
Future criminal or more realistic civil cases need newly researched, versioned
sources for their actual elements, burdens, evidence rules and remedies.

This program does not provide real-case legal advice, legal accreditation or a
validated measure of aptitude. Its deterministic checks test record integrity
and filters, not correctness of every generated question or judicial inference.
