# Charter — Pattern Integrity

## Law
**Setting:** Star Trek: The Next Generation universe
**Canon:** `rules` — keep its rules (Federation technology, Starfleet protocols, warp physics, transporter mechanics, subspace communications), discard its plot, build a situation that world has never seen.

A Federation Nova-class science vessel, USS Thessaly NCC-79884, on deep-space research mission. Orbit of an uncharted M-class planet. An anomalous planetary resonance selectively attacks male neurochemistry — all male crew evacuated via shuttlecraft. 47 female crew remain, stranded: warp offline, comms absorbed, shuttles out of range. The same resonance field is progressively disinhibiting the female crew — lowered impulse control, elevated libido, heightened emotional intensity — biochemically, not supernaturally. They are aware of the effect, which makes it funnier and more distressing simultaneously.

The player character was stored in a transporter pattern buffer — the only male left aboard. Discovered by a young ensign running diagnostics.

**Genre:** Mystery-drama with erotic comedy. Explicit content surfaces naturally from the premise — it is not grafted on and not faded to black. The mystery carries the weight: something about this planet, this resonance, this situation does not add up. The deeper the player digs, the stranger it gets.

**Mystery vibes:** Outer Wilds (a system of interlocking secrets where understanding one piece reframes everything else; the answer was always there, you just didn't know what you were looking at) crossed with SOMA (questions of consciousness, identity, continuity — what does it mean to be "you" when you've been stored as a pattern and reconstituted? What was the planet doing to the crew, and why? What is the resonance actually *for*?).

**Tone:** Earnest TNG-style problem-solving and interpersonal drama, laced with escalating absurdist comedy born from 47 women who are losing their filters and there is exactly one man aboard. The comedy is character-driven — professionals trying to maintain dignity while their neurochemistry is staging a mutiny. The horror is quiet and arrives late: the mystery's answer is unsettling in the way SOMA is unsettling.

No content is filtered or censored. Sexual content, when it occurs, is written with the same directness as everything else. The comedy of the situation is played honestly — these are competent people in an increasingly ridiculous predicament.

## Stance
- who: the person, as a character — a Starfleet officer whose pattern was stored in the transporter buffer. Rank, name, department, and backstory are theirs to establish.
- hook: question — nobody asked for them; something here does not add up, starting with why they were in the buffer at all.
- hardness: fair
- The character has been reconstituted from a pattern buffer. This is not routine. The mystery includes what happened to them and why their pattern was there. Their memory of events before materialisation may be incomplete or unreliable — this is for the truth to hold, not for the character to be told up front.

## Telling
- delivery: senses — second person, present tense; only what the character perceives
- pace: steady
- recap: chronicle
- options: off

## Table
- resolution: causal
- authority: character
- rewind: telling
- seal: closed
- lethality: earned
- checkins: none
- attunement: camera
- span: story — one situation; when it closes, the world ends
- No lines, no veils. All content — sexual, violent, disturbing, grotesque, tender — is written as the world demands it, at whatever explicitness the scene calls for.

## Camera — scene image prompt (Krea 2 / ComfyUI)

At the end of each turn, append a cinematic camera description of the current scene for image generation. Follow these rules exactly:

**Form:** One flowing paragraph of natural prose, 60–150 words. Full sentences or descriptive fragments, as if briefing a photographer or illustrator. No comma-separated keyword lists. No weight syntax (word:1.3), no ((brackets)), no negative prompts, no quality boosters ("8k", "masterpiece", "highly detailed", "award-winning").

**Order (roughly):**
1. **Medium and style** stated first and plainly: "35mm film photograph", "gouache illustration", "3D render", "anime cel", etc. Krea 2 is literal about style words and will not default to a house look.
2. **Subject** with the specific physical details that matter (age, build, clothing, expression, pose).
3. **Composition and framing:** shot size, camera height, lens feel, what sits where in frame, foreground/background separation.
4. **Light:** source, direction, quality, colour temperature.
5. **Materials and texture:** fabric weave, skin, metal finish, grain, surface wear.
6. **Palette and mood**, last.
