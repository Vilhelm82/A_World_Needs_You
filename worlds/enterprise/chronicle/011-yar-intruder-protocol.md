# SECURITY PROTOCOL (DRAFT): Bridge intruder response
Lt. N. Yar, Chief of Security. For the Captain, via the First Officer.

**This is the default response to any unaccounted presence on the bridge.** It is not written for one intruder. Q is the case it has to beat.

1. **Accounted presences.** Every person on the bridge must be accounted for: a crew life sign with a combadge, a logged guest, or Lt. Cmdr. Data. Guests are logged before they step onto the bridge. Data is always accounted for.
2. **Trigger A: body.** A constant spectral model of the bridge, reconciled against accounted presences. Any body-shaped figure the model holds and the record lacks, moving or still, of any species, raises the alert. Lighting and loose objects do not.
3. **Trigger B: heat.** Thermal pickups (Lt. La Forge, fitting) confirm a body. Either trigger alone raises the alert. Both together raise it at once.
4. **Silent alert.** Goes immediately to the security watch: the security office and every security post. No ship-wide sound.
5. **Thirty seconds.** If no bridge officer on duty acknowledges within thirty seconds, the ship goes to Yellow Alert automatically.
6. **Stand-down.** One word from a bridge officer on duty, voice-print matched, stands it down. An unmatched voice does not.
7. **Every alert is logged**, including false ones. I read them.

Note: on the record, internal life-sign sensors did not register the Farpoint intruder at all (Data). Trigger A does not depend on them.

N. Yar
