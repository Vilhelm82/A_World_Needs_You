# Message, Ealat colony infirmary to USS Enterprise (received 1634, forwarded by Dr. Crusher)

To: Dr. B. Crusher, CMO, USS Enterprise
From: Dr. M. Juuso, Javri

The four outside the Ninth.

Two are in Javri households with family on the Ninth. Both visited the summer camp in the week before they fell ill.

One is Aslak Nango, a trader. He buys cheese and milk from the Ninth's siidas and sells them at the Javri market. He goes up to the camp every week with his cart.

One is a girl of six in Javri, Elle Somby. Her family has no one on the Ninth. Nobody in the house has been up there. They buy at the market like everyone else.

The trader's cart goes up and down the valley through the marshes. Reedflies ride in carts; anyone who has driven one knows it. I think that is how it reached the child.

The market is held every seventh day. The next is the day after tomorrow.

Still thirty-three. Still ten.

M. Juuso
