# PROCEDURE: Controlled separation for structural inspection, saucer docking latches 1-12
Engineering. Lt. Cmdr. S. MacDougal, Chief Engineer. For the First Officer.

**Purpose.** Inspect the actuator housings of all twelve docking latches after the emergency separation at Farpoint. Latch 4 carried about 150% of rated load for 0.4 s. Internal sensors nominal; they cannot see the housing.

**Conditions.** Ship at sublight, station-keeping. No traffic within 10,000 km. Battle bridge crewed, conn and ops. First Officer on the saucer for redocking.

**Duration.** 6 hours, including a 2-hour re-inspection allowance. Required minimum 4 hours.

1. Saucer and stardrive crews to stations. Interface utilities isolated.
2. Separation. Stand off to 500 m. (0:30)
3-8. Two suited teams of three, plus the Chief Engineer, on the exposed interface. Structural imaging of housings 1-12, about 20 minutes each, two teams in parallel. (2:00)
9. **HOLD POINT: CHIEF ENGINEER'S CALL.** If imaging shows anything requiring re-inspection, the clock stops. Extension up to 2:00 at the Chief Engineer's discretion. No discussion on open channels.
10. Teams in. Manual redocking from the saucer: approach from 500 m at 0.5 m/s, about 17 minutes plus alignment.
11. Interface re-mate and checks. (1:00)
12. If any housing is cracked: no further separation except in emergency; repair at the next starbase.

Signed: S. MacDougal
Approved: ______________ (Commanding Officer)
