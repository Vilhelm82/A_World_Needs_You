# INTERIM RESTRICTION: saucer docking latch 4
Engineering. Lt. Cmdr. S. MacDougal. For the First Officer, for the Commanding Officer.

Pending inspection of latch 4's actuator housing (see the procedure):

1. No planned saucer separation.
2. In an emergency separation, separate at sublight wherever there is any choice. Separation at warp risks latch 4 failing to release.
3. Inspection within fourteen days, or at the next starbase if sooner. Drydock preferred: no EVA.

Docked, the latch carries nothing it was not built for. No other restriction on the ship's operation.

S. MacDougal
