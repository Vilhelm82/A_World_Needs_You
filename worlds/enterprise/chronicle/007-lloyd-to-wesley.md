# To: Wesley Crusher. From: Cmdr. Lloyd

Mr. Crusher. Sorry for the wait. Today got busy fast, but your message didn't get lost. Bridge passes are the captain's to grant, so I can't promise one. What I can do is put it to him myself once the Hesker IV mission settles down, and I will.

In the meantime, I have a job for you. The colony's records don't tell us where the families on the Ninth pasture draw their water. Pull every survey record we have on that valley: streams, marshes, terrain, where the herds graze. Bring me what you find in my office tomorrow at sixteen hundred. It matters. The away team may use it.

No apology needed for the holodeck. Though I'd stay clear of streams. - Cmdr. Lloyd
