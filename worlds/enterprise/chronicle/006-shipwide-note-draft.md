# Draft: ship-wide note (for the Counselor, then the Captain)

To all crew: The Enterprise is en route to Hesker IV at the request of Starfleet Medical, to assist the colony with an outbreak of an unidentified illness. The ship and crew are healthy. As a precaution, cargo bay four is being prepared as an isolation ward; no decision has been made to bring patients aboard. Further information will follow from command as it becomes available.

- Cmdr. W. Lloyd, First Officer
