# Draft: ship-wide note, revised with the Counselor's line (for the Captain)

To all crew: The Enterprise is en route to Hesker IV at the request of Starfleet Medical, to assist the colony with an outbreak of an unidentified illness. The ship and crew are healthy. As a precaution, cargo bay four is being prepared as an isolation ward; no decision has been made to bring patients aboard. Should the captain decide to, they will be kept entirely separate from the ship's air and its people, and the school and family quarters will not be affected. Counselor Troi's door is open to anyone who would like to talk. Further information will follow from command as it becomes available.

- Cmdr. W. Lloyd, First Officer
