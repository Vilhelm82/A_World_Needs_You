# Security incident log — intruder ("Q"), Farpoint mission
Lt. N. Yar, Chief of Security. For the First Officer.

**1. Bridge, at the barrier.** Intruder appeared in person on the main bridge. Lt. Torres (conn) went for his phaser, set to stun. Intruder froze him solid, phaser in hand. Medics called by ops. Torres to sickbay; since thawed out.
*No intruder alert sounded. No security team dispatched.*

**2. "Courtroom."** Bridge officers removed from the ship to a location the intruder controlled. I disarmed one of his soldiers. Frozen; released.
*No intruder alert sounded. No security team dispatched.*

**3. Bridge, near Deneb IV.** Intruder appeared in person. Ordered off the bridge by the captain. Left when he chose.
*No intruder alert sounded. No security team dispatched.*

**4. Bridge, near Deneb IV, again.** As above.
*No intruder alert sounded. No security team dispatched.*
