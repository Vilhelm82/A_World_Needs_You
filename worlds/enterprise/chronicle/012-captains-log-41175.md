# Captain's log, stardate 41175.9

The Enterprise is at warp eight for Hesker IV, answering a request from Starfleet Medical on behalf of the Ealat colony. An outbreak of an unidentified illness there, with inflammation of the heart, has killed six colonists, four of them children. We arrive late tomorrow. The exploration survey beyond Deneb is suspended.

At this morning's briefing the colony's physician held the illness to be native, carried by an insect from the colony's newest pasture. The first officer asked how a virus found in stool would travel. That question turned Dr. Crusher's attention to the colony's water. We have since learned that the affected families drink from a stream below their herds. A boil-water order is in force there at our advice. We may yet learn that the question was the most useful thing said today.

Preparations: Dr. Crusher has two scientists reassigned to her from the suspended survey, three of Lieutenant Yar's field medics, and replicated cardiac support units under test. A cargo bay is being made into an isolation ward. I have not decided whether any patient will be brought aboard, and I will not until the doctor has seen the virus. A request has gone to Starfleet Medical for a physician and an intensive-care nurse to follow us.

Ship's business: Commander MacDougal suspects that one of the saucer docking latches was damaged in the separation at Farpoint. Until it is inspected, I have restricted separation to emergencies, at sublight where there is any choice. The inspection will be made in clear space after Hesker IV, to her procedure.

Lieutenant Yar has drafted a protocol for intruders on the bridge, and the first officer endorses it. Mr. Data finds that the internal sensors did not register Q as a life sign at any time he was aboard. Lieutenant La Forge's account of what his VISOR showed of him is in Mr. Data's file. I shall read the protocol tonight.

On the first officer's recommendation, a note on the mission went to the crew this afternoon, with a line from Counselor Troi for the families. It appears to have done its work.

Picard out.
