# Scones (Gran's), copied out by S. MacDougal

*Copied as written. Notes in square brackets are mine.*

1 lb flour, self-raising, and a pinch of salt
A good handful of butter, cold [the first officer says forty grams]
2 oz sugar, if it's for company
1 egg
Milk, enough. Not too much.
A handful of sultanas, if you've got them

Rub the butter into the flour till it's like breadcrumbs. Don't warm it with your hands, you'll ruin it. Sugar in, sultanas in. Beat the egg into a cup of milk and add it till it comes together. **Don't knead it.** Pat it out an inch thick, cut them, don't twist the cutter. Hot oven, ten or twelve minutes, till they're risen and gold.

They're best the day you make them. They're not bad the day after either.
