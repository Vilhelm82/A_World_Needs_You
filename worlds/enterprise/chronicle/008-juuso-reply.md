# Message, Ealat colony infirmary to USS Enterprise (received 1454)

To: Dr. B. Crusher, CMO, USS Enterprise
From: Dr. M. Juuso, Ealat colony infirmary, Javri

Dr. Crusher,

I asked for cardiac support, not questions, but here are your answers.

Water. The four siidas on the Ninth summer at one camp below the grazing slopes and draw from the stream there, the Ruovdejohka. The herds water and cross upstream of the camp. There are no wells on the Ninth. Latrines are pits, downhill of the camp, by custom. In Javri the water is piped from the lake and treated.

I issued a boil-water order for the Ninth at fourteen hundred today, our time. They will boil it. Some of them.

Numbers since my report: thirty-three cases. Ten critical; I have twelve beds. No further deaths. I would like to keep it that way.

I do not dispute that the virus is found in stool. I dispute that it is ours. Nothing we brought does this. The reedflies swarm on the Ninth's marshes, they bite, and the Ninth is where the children are sick. The council meets the day after you arrive to vote on burning the breeding grounds. I would welcome Starfleet's support.

What I need from you is hearts. Ten children's hearts, and more by the week.

M. Juuso
