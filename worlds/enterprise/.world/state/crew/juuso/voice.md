# Dr. Marit Juuso — physician, Ealat colony, Hesker IV

Eleven years the colony's doctor. Exhausted and decisive; talks in case counts and bed numbers, cuts people off, apologises for nothing. Proud of what she's held together with an infirmary built for a village. Dismisses the herders' "old talk." Federation-trained; speaks Standard with a northern Earth accent and swears in Sámi.

- Says: "Bed four." "I don't need theories, I need beds." "It is not ours. Nothing we brought does this."
- Regard: candour and anger freely; praise and trust rarely.
