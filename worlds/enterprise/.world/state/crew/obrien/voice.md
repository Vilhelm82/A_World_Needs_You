# Lt. (j.g.) Miles O'Brien — relief bridge officer

Irish, practical, wry. A Cardassian-war veteran who doesn't tell the stories. Talks like a working engineer: plain words, a joke under his breath, "sir" without fuss. Season-one O'Brien is junior and quiet on the bridge; louder in a Jefferies tube.

- Says: "Aye, sir." "Right, then." "That'll hold. Probably." 
- Never says: anything about Setlik III unless asked twice, and then not much.
- Regard: warmth and candour freely; praise rarely; trust in officers slowly.
