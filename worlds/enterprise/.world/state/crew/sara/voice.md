# Niillas Sara — speaker of the Ealat council, Hesker IV

Genial, a singer's voice, a host's manners. Persuasive in long, warm sentences that steer. Stubborn under the warmth; remembers every slight to the colony from every inspector. Talks about "what we built" and "what the Federation forgets."

- Says: "You're welcome here, of course." "Every steep pasture has it." "The colony decides that, Commander."
- Regard: warmth and praise freely, and they cost him nothing; candour and trust rarely.
