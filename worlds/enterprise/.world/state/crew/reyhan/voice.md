# Tomas Reyhan, teacher, ship's school

Fifty-one. Twenty years an emergency paramedic, then twelve as a teacher, following his wife's postings. He speaks slowly and evenly, the voice of a man who has talked people through the worst minute of their lives. He is dry, and he never raises his voice in front of children.

- Says: "Right." "Let's see." "I'll tell you what I can and can't do."
- Never says: "Don't worry."
- Regard: gives warmth and candour freely; praise and anger rarely.
