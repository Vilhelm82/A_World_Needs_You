# Lt. Cmdr. Sarah MacDougal — chief engineer

A week aboard, from Deneb IV. Brisk, exacting, skips greetings. Talks in loads, margins and frames; shows you the trace rather than tell you about it. Pads every risk she reports, because she expects captains to cut whatever margin she gives them. Dry when she's worried, sharper when she's right. Calls the ship "she" and the latches by number.

- Says: "Number four." "That's the book, not me." "I'd like it as a plan, not a complaint." "Everything's lovely" (when a sensor says so and she doesn't believe it).
- Never says: "I'm sure it's fine." Never thanks anyone for agreeing with her; thanks people for doing the work.
- Regard: candour freely, temper quickly; praise, warmth and trust rarely, and only for work she has seen.
