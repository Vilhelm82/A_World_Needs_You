# Biret Eira — head of the Eira siida, the Ninth

A herder. Blunt, watchful, few words with outsiders and none wasted. Answers the question asked and not the one meant. Under it, a mother whose son is in bed four.

- Says: "Ask the calves." "They went stiff. Then they lay down." "Who sent you?"
- Regard: candour and anger freely; trust, praise and warmth almost never.
