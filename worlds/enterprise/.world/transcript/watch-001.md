# Watch 001 — transcript

## turn 1

Main Engineering, a little after the start of alpha shift. The warp core's slow blue pulse climbs the chamber behind the master systems display, and an ensign at the far console is reading a diagnostic aloud to nobody in particular.

Lieutenant Commander MacDougal has the display lit before you reach it. No good morning.

"Docking latch four, sir. Ventral interface, frame thirty-one." She touches the table and twelve strain traces unroll across it, one per latch. Eleven run flat. The fourth spikes, holds, and comes back down. "That's the separation, running from Q. Warp nine and a half, past the red line, which the book says you don't do at any warp. Eleven of them took it. Number four carried half again its rated load for four-tenths of a second."

She lays a second set beside the first. Twelve flat lines.

"And that's your docking at Deneb. Seated, nominal, every sensor happy." She folds her arms. "The sensors are inside the latch mechanism. The actuator housing is round them. If the housing's cracked, they're sitting in the crack telling me everything's lovely."

Over her shoulder: "Ensign, run four again from the top." The ensign does. The same spike climbs the table.

"I can't see that housing with the saucer on. I want a controlled separation at sublight. Six hours, my people out on the ventral hull with a structural imager, and then you put her back the way you did at Deneb." A breath. "I'd like it on the schedule before the captain reads my report. So it lands as a plan, not a complaint."

At her elbow, on a napkin on the console, a replicated scone with one bite out of it.

## turn 2

MacDougal looks at you a moment longer than the order needs. Then at the scone.

"Procedure by eleven hundred." She picks it up, turns it over like a part that failed inspection, and takes a bite. Chews. "My grandmother's. The recipe says a good handful of butter. The replicator wants a number, and every number I give it is wrong." She puts it down. "Order obeyed, sir."

She's already back at the display. "Ensign, pull the full separation checklist from the shakedown and put it on my padd. All of it, not the summary."

"Aye, Commander."

To you: "I'll want the battle bridge crewed for it, and somebody on the saucer who knows what a docking feels like from the inside. If you're flying her back, that's you." A pause. "I'll put six hours in the procedure. I'd rather you didn't let him talk me down to four."
