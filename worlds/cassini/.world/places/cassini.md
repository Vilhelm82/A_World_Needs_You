# USS Cassini, NCC-60349 — Nebula class
Commissioned stardate 39702.0, Utopia Planitia Fleet Yards, Mars. Day 13 of shakedown at open. 480 aboard of 750; departments at shakedown strength; no families.

Dedication plaque, bridge, port side of the turbolift: *"I have observed the highest planet to be threefold."* — Galileo, 1610, misreading Saturn's rings as two moons. The yard's dedication committee chose it; Picard approved it; Data can explain it if asked and will explain more than was asked.

## What she is
A survey cruiser with a mission pod — long-range and low-density survey suite. The pod's mass channel under-reads in media below 0.05 atoms/cm³. It is design, not fault. Nobody aboard knows that; the itinerary calls it an "offset to characterise". Her biomass channel is uncalibrated and reads "indeterminate" past a few thousand kilometres. Certified: phasers, forward transporters. Uncertified: torpedo launchers for live fire; aft transporter pattern buffer (Vorrat does not trust it); pod mass channel. Nursed: port nacelle field coil 3.

Capacity that matters this episode: the pod's survey computer is the only spare computational capacity aboard large enough to run a model the size of the Eldest's. Vorrat knows the capacity exists; nobody has thought of the use.

- XO's quarters, deck 8. Picard's, deck 8, forward.
- Sickbay, deck 12. Prakash's office off it; the chessboard.
- Main engineering, deck 36; Vorrat's office; the cloth on her desk.
- The forward lounge, deck 10. No bartender. Vint has tried the replicator there for his uncle's dish.
- Astrometrics, deck 11 — stellar cartography; Lt. jg ch'Rasseth's station (offstage).

## How she feels
New carpet; warm plastics; a ship not yet lived in. Doors that open a quarter-second late on deck 8 (Vorrat knows; low priority). The crew do not yet finish each other's sentences.
