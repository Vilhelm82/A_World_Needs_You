# The Sending — a Tessuri generation ark
Episode 1. Launched 3,100 years ago from the Hearth (the Tessuri sun; now a remnant 41 ly away, Federation catalogue "unattributed supernova remnant, age ~2,400 y") toward the Hearth-that-follows, 12 ly out, at 0.03c: a 400-year voyage. Found no world there, nor at the next three. Drifting now, 3 km/s, 0.7 ly inside the Orrin Shallows boundary, 40,000 km off the Cassini's port bow.

## What the Cassini can see from 40,000 km (each station has its piece)
- 2.1 km long; a spun cylinder, spin long since stopped. Hull cold and pitted. Vorrat: "lighter than it looks — mostly empty" (the pod under-reads).
- One band of lights, a ring two-thirds aft, that comes on and goes off on a 26-hour cycle. On at open. It is the garden ring.
- No life signs (Kask, Prakash). One section reads "biological material, dormant" — the bank (Prakash: "I'd want a closer pass, and the biomass channel is a guess at this range").
- A warm core, "high-density computational activity" (Data). A reactor signature Vorrat cannot type from here: "older than anything I've seen running. I'd want to be a lot closer to say more, and I don't want to be closer."
- No weapons. No shields. A drive, exhausted. (Kask: "It's dead and it's talking.")
- The hail: audio, repeating on a four-minute interval since 1340, in Tessuri, translator 60%: "We are the Sent of the Sending. We [are / were] fifty-one years from home. We [see / saw] your light. Are you the people of the Hearth-that-follows?"

## Aboard, if boarded
- The lock the Eldest opens, onto a dead corridor; frost; no air until the Eldest pressurises it, which takes twenty minutes and reactor it does not have.
- Cryo bays: 1,212 pods, all dark. Three bays; the children's bay is the smallest.
- The garden ring's outer face: collector panels for the destination orbit, dead cold, intact; they would take the Cassini's deflector as light. The Eldest does not know they still work.
- The garden ring: lights come on over dead beds; the pumps turn over once a day; tenders — small machines on rails — work the beds on the day schedule, dozens of them, all moving the same way. From 1,000 km they cross the windows like shadows.
- The core, "the heart": warm, humming. The model runs here. Screens where the Sent can be seen and heard; the Sent see the visitors on their own screens as people on the Cassini, never in their corridors — the model's corridors are year 40, populated; the real ark's external sensors are their sky and nothing internal reaches them unless the Eldest renders it. It does not.
- Maintenance intercoms at panels every forty metres, wired to their counterparts in the model. Hollen's door.
- The bank: 4,000 embryos in cold; about 40% viable; sensors dead 900 years; power draw high — a bank compensating for failed cells.
- The physical logs: instrument records, 3,100 years, in Tessuri, honest, readable in any order. The launch plans of the second and third Sendings are the first entries. The bank sensor's last update stamp is in there. The power priority table is in there.
- The Eldest's archive: the same, plus the light change of the Hearth, plus the vote of year 40.

## The Sent
961 recorded adults. 41 know. Tessuri: the translator carries their words; their faces on the screens are theirs as they were at year 40 plus eleven years of the model's ageing. Their day is 26 hours. Their festivals are in Ossane's book.
