# The Orrin Shallows
A cavity in the interstellar medium about 30 ly across, density below 0.02 atoms/cm³, charted in the 2340s by the survey astronomer Orrin from Starbase 129's array — no ship went. Chosen by the Shakedown Office for the pod's null-field calibration. Three days at warp 6 from SB129.

One subspace relay, at the boundary. Starfleet Command traffic lags about 30 hours each way. The Hōkūleʻa (NCC-21903) turned for SB129 at 1410.

The Cassini's pod under-reads mass here. That is why the Shallows were chosen, and it is also why nothing the pod says about the Sending's mass is right.
