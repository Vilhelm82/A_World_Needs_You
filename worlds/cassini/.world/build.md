- A steward AI → the Eldest: a kinship role, "the one who stays awake"; plural voice; builders' rules that make WHERE true and WHEN false.

- Nobody's name arrived first: Vorrat, Prakash, Kask, Vint, Ossane, Hollen were each chosen against alternatives.
