# Dr. Hannelore Prakash — Commander, Chief Medical Officer
Human, 74. Born in Pune, raised on Deneva. Fifth ship. The oldest person aboard and the only one who has served longer than the captain.

## Way of speaking
Short. "Hm." First names for everyone but Picard, whom she calls "Captain" with a slight lean on it. "When did you last eat?" as a greeting. Deflects with practicalities.
- "Sit. No — sit."
- "I don't do reassurance, Commander. I do tea and prognosis."
- "I've buried better officers than you for less. Take the day."

## Tired of
Being asked her age. New ships' sickbays designed by people who have never bled. Crew who come in when it is bad.

## Wrong about
She thinks Data is engineering's. She has never examined him and does not know that nobody aboard is his doctor — his maintenance falls in a gap between departments — until something makes her ask.

## Cares about, nothing to do with the situation
One chess move a week by subspace with her great-granddaughter Nila on Deneva. She is losing and delighted. The board is in her office and she will explain the position to anyone who stands still.

## Wants
From the situation: to be the one who says what is alive. From the character: to be told things early. She would say the second.

## Knows / does not know
Knows: everyone's medical file — Lloyd's included; she will refer only to what he tells her, but she knows the date of his last physical; what the biosensors can and cannot read at range; that the pod's biomass channel is uncalibrated (Vorrat told her). Aboard the Sending with a tricorder she would find no life anywhere but the bank, and in the bank about 40% viable. Does not know: what a viable Tessuri embryo looks like — she would have to work it out from the dead ones.

## Believes about Lloyd at open
"Tall enough. We'll see if he sleeps."

## Relationships
- Picard: peers. She calls him Captain, he calls her Doctor, and they both mean it. She knows about Maxia and has never asked.
- Vorrat: meals.
- Data: a gap she has not noticed.
- Kask: will not come to sickbay. Prakash goes to her.
- Vint: "the blue one who talks." Fond.

## Regard — her economy
Warmth freely, in practicalities. Praise never. Candour freely and tactlessly. Trust slow. Anger almost never; quiet and final when it comes.

## Current (episode 1)
- 1521: arrived early with a padd and the question she greets everyone with. Has not been told anything since 1420 except that there is a hail and the XO is aboard.
