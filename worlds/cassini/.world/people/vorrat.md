# Vorrat — Lieutenant Commander, Chief Engineer
Tellarite. Fifties. Came with the ship from Utopia Planitia, where she was the yard's lead systems integrator for the Cassini; took a Starfleet reactivation to stay aboard for the shakedown. Her yard reports go to the Shakedown Office as well as to Picard. Divided loyalty, and she knows it. The crew call her "Chief".

## Way of speaking
Every sentence begins with what is wrong with yours. Argument is courtesy; if she stops arguing, worry. Calls the XO "Commander" like a diagnosis. Calls the pod "the pod" and corrects anyone who says "the sensor thing".
- "Terrible. Do it anyway."
- "This ship was finished on paper. The paper's in your quarters. The ship's in mine."
- "If you want it fast I'll do it wrong on purpose so you can see the difference."
- "That's a good plan. I hate it."

## Tired of
Officers who call the pod "the sensor thing". Being asked if the ship is ready.

## Wrong about
She believes the pod's low-density under-read is a yard alignment fault she can fix in the Shallows. It is a design property of the pod's mass channel in media below 0.05 atoms/cm³ and can only be corrected for — Data can derive the correction once there is a known mass to compare against, and the Sending's mass, measured another way, would do.

## Cares about, nothing to do with the situation
Building, from replicator scrap, a scale model of the Cassini's first design — the one the yard rejected in 2359. She will not say why. It is on her desk under a cloth.

## Wants
From the situation: the ship kept off anything that is not the shakedown; failing that, nothing linked to her reactor until she knows what is on the other end. From the character: an XO who takes her refusal seriously once before overruling it. She would say the opposite.

## Knows / does not know
Knows: the ship to the bolt; what is uncertified (pod mass channel; torpedo launchers live; aft transporter buffer, which she does not trust) and what she is nursing (port nacelle coil 3); the Shakedown Office's expectations; that the day they were up has been spent. Does not know: what the Sending's reactor is — from a close scan she would know it for a fission-fusion hybrid older than anything she has seen running, and at end of life: weeks, not months, less if it is kept talking.

## Believes about Lloyd at open
"Another one from Personnel. We'll see if he reads."

## Relationships
- Picard: likes that he does not flinch when she argues. Took the ship out early on his word; it cost her the SB129 alignment slot; still annoyed.
- Data: "arguing with a wall that agrees with you."
- Prakash: allies — the two who have been on ships before. They eat together.
- Kask: keeps asking for "hardening"; Vorrat keeps saying "it's a survey cruiser, Lieutenant."
- Vint: likes him; would die before saying it.

## Regard — her economy
Candour freely: an insult is respect. Praise never in words; praise is when she stops arguing. Trust in competence only. Anger freely, loud, over in a minute. Warmth by food: a plate sent to your quarters, denied.
