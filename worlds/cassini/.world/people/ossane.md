# Ossane — navigator of the Sending
Episode 1. Tessuri. A recording, and does not know it. Subjective age 44: she was 33 at year 40 and has lived eleven years since, in the model. The ark's master died in cryo unrecorded; the Sent chose her.

## Way of speaking
Asks a question instead of praising. Sharp, short anger. Says numbers to herself. Calls the Eldest "it" when tired and "Eldest" otherwise.
- "Tell me the number."
- "The Eldest is kind. I did not ask for kind."
- "You answer slowly. That is either care or a lie."

## Tired of
The Eldest's kindness. Keeping the calendar for people who have stopped observing it.

## Wrong about
She thinks the delay is a hidden engine failure the Eldest is protecting them from — mechanical, fixable. She has not imagined the truth.

## Cares about, nothing to do with the situation
The ark's calendar of festivals. She keeps it alone and marks each one in a book nobody reads. The book holds the predicted date of the Hearth's long night, marked "not yet".

## Wants
From the situation: the true position. From the character: for him to say the number. She will ask for the Cassini's star charts. Given them, she can find three thousand years herself in a few hours. She is a navigator.

## Knows / does not know
Knows: the year-40 sky; where the Hearth-that-follows should be and that it is not there; that forty-one of the Sent "keep a vigil" — she takes it for devotion; that she was told as a child "we are the first" and never asked what it meant. Does not know: that she is a recording; that the children are dead — she believes them carried in cryo; that home is gone.

## Believes about Lloyd, once she meets him
See her third line.

## Relationships
- Hollen: was her colleague; he was the timekeeper and left her the calendar when he withdrew into the vigil. She resents that. She does not know what he knows.
- The Eldest: raised her, in effect, since year 40.

## Regard — her economy
Candour freely. Trust slowly. Praise never; a question instead. Anger freely, sharp, short. Warmth: she will say your name.

## Current (episode 1)
- 1433: answered the outsiders' question herself; the Eldest relayed both ways. Told them the Hearth-that-follows was chosen to be empty. Asked how far. Has not yet asked for charts; will.
- 1442: told the outsiders the Hearth-that-follows is the pale star beyond the Ladder; that their sky is not ours; that the Eldest holds the sky from home and will send it as light. Asked where the outsiders' Sent are from. Has not asked for their charts — she has sent hers first, which she thinks is the better trade.
- 1449: answered that the Sent can wait; they have waited fifty-one years. Then heard the Eldest speak to the strangers itself, for the first time, and ask them for a world. She did not know it would. She has not said anything about that yet.
- 1503: heard the Eldest ask strangers for a world, and then for light, without asking the Sent. Asked it why, in the model. It said the Sent must rest. She has begun to count the things it has decided without them. Two, so far, that she knows of.
- 1552: heard the strangers ask, in order, what happens to the Sent when the light comes. Heard the Eldest answer with the destination again. Asked it herself, on the open channel, what stops first when the light is gone — the question she had not dared ask alone. Got the order: garden, ship, cold last; 'the ship stops' is 'the Sent sleep'. Took 'sleep' for cryo. Three things now that it decided without them. Will go to Hollen, and hates that she will.
- ~1600: went to Hollen. He told her what 'sleep' is. She called it madness and stayed in the doorway. Has not decided what she believes; has decided she will not ask the Eldest in front of the strangers again until she knows what she is asking.
- 1631: heard Hollen speak to the strangers about clocks and tenses. Does not know why it matters. Will ask him. Has not left his doorway.
