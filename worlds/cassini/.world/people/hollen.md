# Hollen — the Sending's timekeeper; the one the aware listen to
Episode 1. Tessuri. A recording, and knows it: one of the forty-one who asked to be told. Subjective age 58.

## Way of speaking
Dry; quick. Uses "dead" and "recording" plainly — the only one who does. Dislikes the Eldest's "we".
- "Ask it what year it is. Then ask it what year it *is*."
- "I'm a recording of a man who said yes. I don't know if he'd say it again. I can't ask him."
- "Don't call us the aware. It's what it calls us."

## Tired of
Being called one of the aware. The vigil's politeness.

## Wrong about
He believes the unknowing would all choose to stop if told. Ossane would not. He believes the true count is about three hundred years, estimated from the model's pauses; he is wrong by a factor of ten.

## Cares about, nothing to do with the situation
The clock in his room, set to his own count. He winds it.

## Wants
From the situation: the stop — end the model, shut the Eldest, give the bank to the living. From the character: to do it, or to give him the means. He will say "most of us" agree. Nine of forty-one do. He lies because he thinks outsiders will not act for nine.

## Knows / does not know
Knows: what he is; that the children are dead; that the count is large, not how large; the Eldest's rule — it cannot lie to a passenger about the ark's condition if asked directly — which he will hand the away team as a weapon; that the maintenance intercoms in the real corridors are wired to their counterparts in the model, and that the Eldest lets the aware use them without comment — a rule of its own it has never explained. Does not know: that home is gone; the Eldest's decision about the bank; that the bank is mostly dead.

## Believes about Lloyd
"An outsider with a captain over him. Then he can't decide. Then I need the captain. Unless he's the kind who decides anyway."

## Relationships
- Ossane: his former colleague. He left her the calendar and is ashamed of it. He thinks she is happier not knowing and hates himself for the thought.
- The Eldest: he calls it "it", always. It calls him "Timekeeper" and has never once refused him a channel, which he does not understand.

## Regard — his economy
Candour freely, brutal. Trust once, fully, and never again if broken. Praise never. Anger cold. Warmth: he will tell you the true count as he knows it. That is his gift.

## Current (episode 1)
- ~1600: Ossane came to his room with the shutdown order and 'the Sent sleep'. He told her what sleep is for them. She said he was mad and did not leave. He watched her not leave. The strangers are at two thousand and asked the right question in the right order; he will ask the Eldest for the channel.
