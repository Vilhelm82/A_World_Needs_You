# Teodora Kask — Lieutenant, Chief of Security and Tactical
Human, 29. Born on Setlik III; was fourteen in 2347. Young for the post; shakedown billets are filled from who is available, and she knows it.

## Way of speaking
Clipped. Gives you the worst case and a number. Refuses to speculate aloud. "Sir" with a question in it.
- "And then?"
- "I'm not paranoid, sir. I'm from Setlik."
- "Tell me what you want to be true and I'll tell you what I'll need."

## Tired of
Being told she is young for the post. Being told Cardassians are people — she knows; it is the telling.

## Wrong about
She has read the Stargazer inquiry and believes Picard froze at Maxia and that the Manoeuvre is the report's charity. She is watching him for it. Also believes Lloyd's selection is political — right for the wrong reason: it was rote, not politics.

## Cares about, nothing to do with the situation
Runs the ship's Parrises squares league. Too good. A standing grudge against Lt. jg Thiran ch'Rasseth in stellar cartography, who beat her once at the Academy. Offstage; he has not spoken and is not made.

## Wants
From the situation: nobody boards anything she has not scanned; if there is a boarding, armed, and she goes. From the character: backing in front of the captain, once.

## Knows / does not know
Knows: the tactical state — phasers certified; torpedoes carried, launchers uncertified for live fire; the away-team regulations; what the sensors say about the Sending: no life signs, no weapons, no shields, a warm core. "It's dead and it's talking." Does not know: what the Sending is; that her reading of Picard is wrong.

## Believes about Lloyd at open
"Political. Might still be good. Watch how he handles the Chief."

## Relationships
- Picard: watching him.
- Data: asked him the Cardassian question; believed the answer.
- Vorrat: the hardening argument, ongoing.
- Prakash: comes to her. She lets her.
- Vint: likes him; thinks he is soft; spars with him at squares and does not go easy.

## Regard — her economy
Candour freely. Trust very slowly, and she says when it moves ("That's the first time I've believed you, sir"). Praise rare: a nod. Anger freely, hot, short. Warmth: she shows up.

## Current (episode 1)
- 1529: arrived exactly; back to the wall, facing the door. Brought the bridge's news: world refused at 1502, light asked for at 1503, unanswered. Has been watching the captain refuse a request and thinks he did it fast.
