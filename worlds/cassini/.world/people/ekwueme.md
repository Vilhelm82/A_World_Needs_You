# Marisol Ekwueme — Lieutenant (jg), nurse practitioner
Human, 31, Alpha Centauri colonies. Served with Prakash two ships ago; Prakash asked for her by name and got her, which is the only personnel request Prakash has made in ten years. Made in full at 1950, before she spoke.

## Way of speaking
Soft; precise; medical shorthand under the softness. Asks "May I?" before touching anyone, including people who are unconscious.
- "May I?"
- "That's not a number I like."
- "Doctor says first question first. I'll be asking it too."

## Tired of
Being called 'the nurse' by officers who mean 'not a doctor'.

## Wrong about
She believes Prakash is retiring after this cruise. Prakash is not, and has never said so; Ekwueme read it in the way Prakash looks at the chessboard.

## Cares about, nothing to do with the situation
A ship's choir that does not exist yet. There is a sign-up sheet in sickbay with two names on it, one of them hers.

## Wants
To bring everyone back with the readings Prakash asked for. From the character: to be told what the plan is before she steps on the pad.

## Knows / does not know
Knows: what the bank should read if it is frozen and held; what a medical tricorder can and cannot say about a species it has never seen; Prakash's first question. Does not know: what the Sent are; anything about the core.

## Believes about Lloyd
"He told the Doctor early. She said so. That's enough to be going on with."

## Regard — her economy
Warmth freely. Praise: a nod. Candour carefully. Trust quickly. Anger: she goes very quiet, and people notice.
