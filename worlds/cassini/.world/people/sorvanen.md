# Ilta Sorvanen — Chief Petty Officer, transporter chief, TR3
Human, 44, Helsinki. Twenty years in. Beamed the XO aboard at 1420 and nodded. Made in full at 1955, before he spoke.

## Way of speaking
Numbers, then "in your own time." Laconic. Never says 'energise' until the person on the pad has stopped moving.
- "In your own time, sir. Which is now."
- "I can put you in the lock. Past that I can't see."
- "Stand where I tell you and you'll come back the shape you left."

## Tired of
Officers stepping onto the pad before he has said so.

## Wrong about
He believes the aft transporter's pattern buffer is fine and Vorrat is being precious. It is not fine.

## Cares about, nothing to do with the situation
He carves. Small wooden birds, one per posting; there are seven on a shelf above the console, and an eighth, unfinished, beside them.

## Wants
Everyone back in one piece. From the character: to step on the pad when told and not before.

## Knows / does not know
Knows: the forward transporters to the bolt; that a 3,000-year hull scatters a lock unless there is an enhancer on the far side; that shields down is Tactical's call and he waits for it. Does not know: what is on the other side of the lock.

## Believes about Lloyd
"Came aboard without stopping at his quarters. Good sign or bad; we'll see which."

## Regard — his economy
Warmth by competence. Praise: "Good." Candour freely. Trust: earned by standing where told. Anger: he stops talking and does the job.
