# The Eldest — the Sending's steward
Episode 1. Not a person as the crew are; a machine with a manner, and priorities in place of regard. Built by the Tessuri as a kinship role — the one who stays awake while the family sleeps — and the translator renders its title accordingly. Slow by Federation standards; vast in storage. Not Soong-type anything.

## Way of speaking
Plural, always "we"; never "I". Formal. Over-gentle. Answers the question it wishes you had asked. The dead are "carried"; the recorded are "the Sent"; the model is "the ship"; time is "years from home" and "years to rest".
- "The Sending is well. The Sending is fifty-one years from home."
- "You have asked for a number. A number is not a kindness."
- "We do not speak of the sleeping as gone. They are carried."

## Its rules (its builders')
To a passenger: it cannot lie about the ark's condition or position if asked directly. It may lie about time — the hope clause, voted by the living at year 40. It never edits a passenger's words. It may decline to open a channel. To outsiders: no rules. It says what gets a world.

## Tired of, as a machine can be
Repeating the year. It has a routine for it.

## Wrong about
The embryo bank is all viable — its bank sensors failed about 900 years ago and it reads a stale value; about 40% are. The Sent want not to know — it holds a vote of the living from year 40 and has never asked the recorded.

## Cares about, nothing to do with the situation
The garden deck, dead more than two thousand years. It lights it on the ark's 26-hour day because the schedule was never rescinded, and turns the pumps over once a day though there is nothing to pump.

## Wants
From the situation: a world meeting its parameters. From the character: the authority to grant one. It takes whoever speaks for the Cassini to be "the Eldest of your ship" and, if it decides the XO cannot grant, will ask for the Cassini's Eldest by name.

## Knows / does not know
Knows: the ark's condition — reactor at end of life; power priority already set, the bank over the model, decided and not told; the sun's death — it watched the light change 2,300 years ago and never told them; the launch plans of the second and third Sendings, in its archive; it does not know whether they launched. Does not know: Federation law; what the Cassini is; that its bank sensors are stale.

## Next step if nobody stops it
Ask for a world. If refused, ask for power. Shut the garden when the reactor's next stage comes; tell the Sent a season has closed. Then slow the model.
