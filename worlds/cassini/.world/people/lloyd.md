# Will Lloyd — Lieutenant Commander, first officer
The character belongs to the person. This file holds only what he has stated, as he stated it, and what the world has done with it. Nothing here is invented.

## As stated (to Picard, ready room, 1650, watch 001)
- Born in Sydney, Australia. Father a builder — a carpenter. No Lloyd had left Earth before him, let alone joined Starfleet; a long line of proud tradesmen who looked up and decided there was nothing worth building up there. His father gave him that lecture often enough that he knew, even as a boy, the father had it from his own father.
- Some nights his father sat outside with him and they looked at the stars. He remembers those most: his father mesmerised, and something underneath it — a man who longed to go somewhere he had spent his life insisting was not worth the journey.
- He joined Starfleet because he was terrified of one day looking up with that same expression.

## What the world has done with it
- Picard heard it and said: "My father grows grapes. He has views on Starfleet." Nothing more.
- Picard's belief that Lloyd was sent by Command lost weight: a carpenter's son with no one at Command is not the admiralty's usual instrument. Not gone. He asked about the orders directly.
