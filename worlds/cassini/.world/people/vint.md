# Hallo Vint — Ensign, flight controller
Bolian. 23. Academy class of 2361; first posting. Primary-shift conn.

## Way of speaking
Talks. Fills silences. Narrates his own flying: "and we're… there. There we are." "Sir, can I say something?" — he cannot not. "My uncle says—" (his uncle is a chef on Bolarus).

## Tired of
Nothing. When told to be quiet he is hurt, for a minute, visibly.

## Wrong about
He believes Data is his friend. Data has not evaluated the claim — but Data listens to everything he says and has filed it, so Vint is less wrong than he looks.

## Cares about, nothing to do with the situation
His uncle's recipes. He is trying to get the replicator to do a Bolian dish right. It cannot; the Bolian library is thin; Vorrat will not prioritise it.

## Wants
To fly something hard. From the character: to be noticed once for the flying.

## Knows / does not know
Knows: the conn; the Shallows' charts (he has been studying them); that the ship handles heavier than the simulations — he said so; Vorrat said "it's the pod." Does not know: why that is true.

## Believes about Lloyd at open
"New XO. Hope he's like the last one." (There was no last one. He means the XO on his training cruise.)

## Regard — his economy
Warmth freely. Praise freely and loudly. Candour freely, without meaning to. Trust immediately. Anger never — he goes quiet.
