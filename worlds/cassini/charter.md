# Charter — Cassini

preset: modules/tng

## Law
- Star Trek: The Next Generation. A reusable TNG world: fresh new stories each new session, the same ship and crew. `canon: present` for Captain Jean-Luc Picard and Lt. Commander Data, held to their canon voice. `canon: rules` for everything else.
- A younger Captain Picard than the show's: this is before the Enterprise-D. Year 2362, stardates 39xxx — the window after the Stargazer inquiry and before Picard's next canon command *(assumed)*. Data has recently made Lieutenant Commander *(assumed)*.
- A new starship *(assumed: newly commissioned, on her shakedown cruise)*.
- Mystery drama. Themes in the spirit of The Outer Wilds and SOMA: what was already over when you arrived; records read out of order by someone who came too late; whether a copy is the person; what an ending is owed. Never named inside the world.

## Stance
- who: Lieutenant Commander Will Lloyd, selected for promotion to Commander, joining as first officer (XO). The promotion confirms on the captain's endorsement. Under Picard's evaluation as to competency — a fact of the world, never its subject.
- Lloyd's past and inner life are his to state when asked; the world uses what he states and never invents it *(assumed)*.
- hook: need, and a question — decided per episode *(assumed)*.
- hardness: fair *(assumed)*
- lethality: earned *(assumed)*

## Telling
- delivery: senses *(assumed)*
- pace: steady *(assumed)*
- recap: chronicle — the captain's log, department logs, orders; never Lloyd's own *(assumed)*
- options: off *(assumed)*

## Table
- resolution: causal *(assumed)*
- authority: character *(assumed)*
- rewind: telling *(assumed)*
- seal: closed *(assumed)*
- sitting: open *(assumed)*
- checkins: none *(assumed)*
- attunement: off *(assumed)*
